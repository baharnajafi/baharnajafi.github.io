Case study · eligibility knowledge graph

Immigration eligibility rules as a knowledge graph

At an early-stage startup, I designed the ontology and graph model for immigration eligibility: a shared layer that defines concepts such as "one year of skilled work" once, and a Neo4j subgraph per stream that references it. A declarative condition language and a tri-state evaluator tell a person which streams they qualify for and what is still missing.
