How a dangling edge gets resolved

An edge that names a device with no node isn't a crash — it's a traceable signal. This is the real mechanism: how the vision model's per-device connection claims become a clean asset graph, how phantom endpoints are resolved deterministically, and where a targeted re-query picks up the rest. The insight: recognizing “X connects to Y” and recognizing “Y exists” are two independent acts of recall for a vision model. The consolidation step is designed to tolerate the gap, resolve most of it deterministically, and flag the rest for a cheap, targeted re-query — instead of brute-force re-running the whole document.
